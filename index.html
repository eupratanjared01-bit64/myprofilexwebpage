Dearest June Bless,

Happy 30th birthday. I know I'm not there to say this in person, and I know no screen or message can fully carry the weight of what I want to tell you. But please, read this slowly — because every word comes from a place of deep respect, admiration, and love.

You chose to leave home. Not because you wanted to, but because you decided that our future mattered more than your comfort. You packed your courage into a suitcase, swallowed your fear, and built a life in a place where no one knew your name — all so that we could stand a little taller back home.

I want you to know that I see it. I see the early mornings and the late nights. I see the holidays you spent away from us, smiling through video calls while your heart ached for home. I see the way you send help without being asked, check on us without being reminded, and worry about us even when you should be resting.

You have become more than a sister to us. You became the mother who stayed, even when she had to leave. The provider who gave without keeping score. The woman who held this family together from across the world — not with her hands, but with her will, her love, and her unwavering sense of duty.

But today, on your 30th birthday, I want to ask you to do something you rarely do: turn that love inward. You have poured so much of yourself into us. Now, pour some of it into yourself. Rest when your body asks. Say yes to the things that bring you peace. Build a life abroad that isn't just about survival or sacrifice — but about you. Your joy. Your growth. Your dreams.

You are thirty years old. You have already done more for your family than most people do in a lifetime. That is not an exaggeration — it is a fact. And the fact that you did it quietly, without demanding recognition, only makes it more extraordinary.

Please remember this: the distance between us is measured in miles, not in love. No ocean is wide enough to separate what you've built in our hearts. You are with us in every decision we make, every lesson we carry, every moment we choose to be strong — because you taught us what strength looks like.

Keep going, June. But also, be gentle with yourself. You are not just a provider, not just a mother figure, not just a worker abroad. You are a woman who deserves softness, celebration, and a life that gives back to her as much as she has given to the world.

We are so proud of you. And we are waiting for you — not just to come home, but to come home to yourself. 💜

With all my love and admiration,
Your brother
